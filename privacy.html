Privacy

Last updated 11 August 2026

Short version: the only thing collected is what you type into the sign-up form, and it is only ever used to tell you about music. It is never sold and never handed to anyone else to market to you.

What gets collected

  • Your email address, when you type it into the Inner Circle form.
  • Your first name and phone number, but only if you choose to add them. Both are optional and you can join without either.
  • The date and time you agreed, and the exact wording you agreed to. This is kept as a record that you said yes.
  • The page you signed up from, and the site that referred you, if any.

Nothing else. There is no account, no password, and no payment information anywhere on this site.

What it's used for

  • Telling you about new music, videos, and shows.
  • Nothing else. No third-party advertising, no data brokers, no resale. Your details are never rented, sold, or shared for anyone else's marketing.

Texts

If you give a phone number and tick the consent box, you'll get recurring automated marketing texts from PRXD.JAY. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of buying anything.

Reply STOP to any message to stop them permanently. Reply HELP for help. Carriers do not charge for those two replies, though your normal message rates still apply.

Your phone number is never shared with anyone for their own marketing, and mobile opt-in data is never sold or passed to third parties for any purpose.

Email

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off, permanently. You don't have to give a reason.

Getting your details deleted

Email prxd.jay@gmail.com and ask. Everything held about you gets deleted. No questions, no delay, no attempt to talk you out of it.

Who else touches it

The list is stored with the email and text services used to send the messages. They process it only to deliver what you asked for, and are not permitted to use it for anything of their own.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no ad-network trackers. It stores a small amount of information in your own browser — whether you've already joined, which look you picked, whether you dismissed a prompt — so it doesn't ask you the same thing twice. That never leaves your device and clearing your browser data removes it.

Music and video players embedded from Spotify and YouTube are loaded from those companies and are covered by their own privacy policies. Videos load nothing at all until you tap play.

Age

This site isn't aimed at children under 13, and information isn't knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
