Privacy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Short version: this site does not currently run a sign-up list or ask you for personal information.

Information you choose to send

If you email PRXD.JAY or send a direct message through a linked social platform, the information you send is used to respond to you. It is not sold or shared for someone else's marketing.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no ad-network trackers. It stores a small amount of information in your own browser, including your color choice and whether you have entered the site before. That information never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Music and video players from Spotify and YouTube are covered by those companies' own privacy policies. YouTube videos do not load until you tap play.

Links to other services

This site links to music, video, social, and commerce platforms. When you follow one of those links, that service's privacy policy applies.

Age

This site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
