Privacy

Last updated 12 August 2026

This page explains what this site collects, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

Email list

When the email list is available, the form collects the email address you enter, the time of the request, and the place on this site where you signed up. A confirmation email is sent before the address is added to the active list.

The site does not currently collect phone numbers. If text-message signup is added later, this policy and the sign-up language will be updated before collection begins.

How the information is used

  • To send new music, video, and show announcements you asked to receive.
  • To confirm signup requests, process unsubscribes, prevent abuse, and maintain the list.

Contact information is not sold, rented, or shared with other companies for their own marketing.

Service providers

Cloudflare protects and serves the site and processes sign-up requests. Brevo stores confirmed email contacts and sends list messages. These services process information to provide those functions under their own terms and privacy commitments.

Browser storage and media

This site does not use advertising trackers. Cloudflare Web Analytics may collect page-view and performance measurements without collecting personal data or tracking people across websites. The site may also store simple preferences in your browser, such as whether you have confirmed a signup or dismissed a prompt. Email addresses and phone numbers are not stored in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes these preferences.

Spotify and YouTube media are provided by those companies. Their players may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when they load. YouTube videos are not loaded until you choose to play one.

Unsubscribe, access, and deletion

Every list email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email prxd.jay@gmail.com to ask what contact information is held about you, correct it, or request deletion.

Retention and security

Confirmed contact information is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, unless a longer period is required for legal or abuse-prevention reasons. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Age

This site is not directed to children under 13, and information is not knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
